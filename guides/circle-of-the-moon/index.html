Chapters

Each stop ends at a boss. Afterward, loop back for power-ups your new relic unlocks.

01

Catacomb

Climb out of the opening pit, learn the whip and sub-weapons, and map the early side rooms. Defeat Cerberus for the Double relic (double jump).

  • Clear both forks before the boss when you can — early hearts and cards matter.
  • Double jump opens Abyss Stairway and the climb to Audience Room.
02

Audience Room

Ascend Abyss Stairway, cross the Outer Wall and Triumph Hallway. Defeat Necromancer for Tackle (break heavy blocks).

  • Use Tackle on cracked walls and floor blocks you passed earlier.
  • Machine Tower is next — expect gears, spikes, and vertical pressure.
03

Machine Tower

Push through conveyor hazards and armored foes. Defeat Iron Golem for the Kick Boots (wall kick).

  • Wall kick reaches high shafts and missed chests in earlier towers.
  • Eternal Corridor leads into Chapel Tower.
04

Chapel Tower

Cross Eternal Corridor and scale the chapel. Defeat Adramelech, then destroy the blocking statues that open the deeper castle.

  • After statues fall, you can commit to the required Waterway path — or detour optional.
  • Gallery → Warehouse → Arena need Gallery first.
07

Underground Waterway

Navigate flooded corridors (poison water unless you cleared Death). Defeat Camilla for the Roc Wing (flight / high jump).

  • Roc Wing is the late-game exploration key — revisit locked ceilings everywhere.
  • Observation Tower opens for the duel with Hugh.
08

Observation Tower

Climb the tower, gather late DSS cards and gear, then defeat Hugh for the Last Key.

  • Last Key unlocks the Ceremonial Room path to Dracula.
  • Optional Battle Arena wants Heavy Ring + Roc Wing.
10

Ceremonial Room

Use the Last Key, prepare DSS combos and healing, then defeat Dracula to finish Nathan’s story.

  • Bring strong offense cards and room to dodge wide sweeps.
  • Save before the door — classic CotM final check.

Optional wings

Skip these on a first clear if you want a lean path — or take them for relics and XP.

Underground Gallery

Dragon Zombie. Required if you want Warehouse or Battle Arena. Strong XP and card hunting.

Underground Warehouse

Death → Heavy Ring. Cleanses Waterway poison and unlocks heavy-object puzzles.

Battle Arena

Seventeen-room trial. Needs Heavy Ring and Roc Wing. Repeatable challenge run.

Playing tips

Small habits that keep CotM’s difficulty fair.

  • Farm DSS cards early — Saturn / Pluto / Mercury combos carry mid-game.
  • After every relic, backtrack: CotM hides many upgrades behind “now you can reach that ledge.”
  • Don’t sequence-break your first run; optional bosses are easier with Roc Wing later, but Gallery first if you want them.
  • Save often at candles / rooms with MP — boss retries are part of the loop.
